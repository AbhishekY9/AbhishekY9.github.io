Profile

Abhishek Yadav is a Research Scholar in the Department of Ocean Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Madras (IIT Madras), Chennai, India. He obtained his B.Tech. degree in Petroleum Engineering from the Rajiv Gandhi Institute of Petroleum Technology (RGIPT), Amethi, India, and later joined IIT Madras for his M.Tech. programme, which was upgraded to a Ph.D. His research focuses on geothermal reservoir modeling, integrating fluid flow, heat transfer, and geomechanical processes in porous media. He also works on optimization and predictive modeling using machine learning (ML), deep learning (DL), and recurrent neural networks (RNN).
